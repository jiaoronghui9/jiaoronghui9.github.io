Product Overview

High Adsorption, Hard Particles, Honest Grades

Coal-based activated carbon offers the best balance of adsorption capacity, hardness and cost for water and gold applications. Granular carbon (8x30 mesh) fills fixed-bed filters for dechlorination, odor removal and final polishing in drinking water plants; powdered carbon (200 mesh) dosing handles seasonal taste, odor and algae events.

In gold mining — a major industry across Indonesia and Vietnam — our granular carbon serves CIL/CIP circuits with high hardness (≥90-95%) to resist attrition and gold loss. We supply two honest grades (Iodine 800 and 1000) with every batch COA-tested for iodine number, methylene blue and hardness — no grade blending, no spec games.

FAQ

Activated Carbon — Common Questions

Iodine 800 or 1000 — which grade should I buy?
Iodine number tracks adsorption capacity for small molecules like chlorine and iodine-scale organics. For municipal dechlorination and general polishing, Iodine 800 is usually sufficient and more economical. For gold CIL/CIP circuits, organics-heavy water, or where vendors demand premium specs, Iodine 1000 earns its price. Tell us your application and we will recommend honestly — we will not upsell capacity you cannot use.
Is your carbon suitable for gold mining CIL/CIP?
Yes — our Iodine 1000 granular carbon (8x30 mesh) offers hardness ≥95% to minimize attrition losses in carbon-in-leach and carbon-in-pulp circuits, plus fast gold adsorption kinetics. We ship to gold mining regions in Indonesia regularly, with COA covering iodine, hardness and mesh distribution. Samples are available for adsorption testing with your ore solution.
Can I mix activated carbon with PAC/PAM in one order?
Absolutely — carbon is a natural add-on to the same container as PAC, PAM and PFS for water treatment customers. One supplier, one invoice, one customs document set for Vietnam or Indonesia import.
