Product Overview

Anionic PAM for Efficient Flocculation

Anionic polyacrylamide (APAM) is a water-soluble high polymer carrying negative charge groups. It adsorbs and bridges suspended particles in water, forming large, fast-settling flocs — the workhorse flocculant for coal washing, sand washing, mineral tailings, municipal sewage and industrial effluent treatment.

Sourced from long-term partner factories in Gongyi's water treatment chemical industrial belt, we supply seven molecular weight grades (6 to 25 million) with hydrolysis degree 10-35% — matched to your water quality by our technical team, with free samples for jar testing before you commit to a full order.

FAQ

Anionic PAM — Common Questions

Which molecular weight and hydrolysis degree should I choose?
It depends on your suspended solids, particle size and pH. As a rule: coal/sand washing uses high MW (16-25 million); municipal sewage works well at 8-12 million. Send us your water source and turbidity — our technical team will recommend the right grade and provide free samples for jar testing.
Can I get a sample before ordering?
Yes — free samples of 500g-1kg, shipped within 2-3 working days. You only cover the courier fee (DHL/FedEx, approximately $30-50). We can include COA and MSDS with the sample for your evaluation.
What is the MOQ and delivery time?
MOQ is 1 ton (25kg bags or 1000kg jumbo bags). Standard grades ship in 7-10 days after deposit; delivery to Ho Chi Minh or Jakarta takes an additional 3-10 days depending on port. Form E certificate provided for ASEAN preferential tariff.
