Product Overview

Simple, Effective Chlorination

Sodium hypochlorite is the workhorse disinfectant of water treatment: dosed as a liquid straight into the process, it destroys bacteria and viruses, oxidizes odor compounds, and maintains a measurable residual chlorine through the distribution network. It is the standard choice for municipal water plants, building and pool systems, and effluent disinfection before discharge.

We supply NaOCl ≥10% solution in 1000L IBC tanks and 25L/30L/200L drums, MOQ 20 tons (one full container of IBCs). Because hypochlorite strength decays over time, we ship fresh production and state the production date on every unit — batch COA with exact available chlorine is included. FAQ

Sodium Hypochlorite — Common Questions

How fast does sodium hypochlorite lose strength?
Strength decays with time, temperature and sunlight — roughly a few percent per month at moderate temperatures, faster in heat and light. That is why we ship fresh production in opaque IBCs/drums, print the production date on every unit, and recommend planning your inventory to use the product within 1-2 months of arrival for full-strength dosing.
How should it be stored and handled?
Store in a cool, shaded, ventilated area with sealed containers — never mix with acids or ammonia compounds (toxic chlorine gas can form). Handling requires standard PPE (gloves, goggles) per the MSDS we provide with every shipment. Your local dosing equipment in compatible plastics (PE/PVC) needs no modification.
Can sodium hypochlorite ship together with other chemicals?
Sodium hypochlorite is an oxidizer, so shipping regulations require it segregated from acids and organic materials in the same container. It can typically be loaded alongside our inorganic salts like PAC powder and sodium acetate with proper segregation — confirm with our team when planning a mixed container and we will handle the stowage plan correctly.
