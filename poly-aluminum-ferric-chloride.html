Product Overview

Two Coagulants in One Product

PAFC merges the charge neutralization of polyaluminum chloride with the dense-floc, wide-pH performance of iron salts. In practice that means: faster floc formation on high-turbidity surface water, good performance where plain PAC struggles (low temperature, color, algae-laden raw water), and heavy-metal co-precipitation borrowed from iron chemistry.

It is a natural fit for Southeast Asian river water, which swings hard between dry-season clarity and monsoon turbidity — one product covers both seasons instead of switching chemicals. We supply PAFC powder with Al2O3+Fe ≥18%, packed in 25kg bags, MOQ 1 ton. Detailed grade specification and batch COA are provided with every shipment — ask us for the datasheet.

FAQ

PAFC — Common Questions

Why choose PAFC over standard PAC?
If your raw water is stable and moderate, standard PAC is usually the most economical choice. PAFC earns its place when water is difficult: high or wildly varying turbidity, low winter temperatures that slow aluminum hydrolysis, stubborn color, or heavy metals that iron co-precipitates well. A jar test comparing both on your water settles it — we supply free samples of each.
Can you provide detailed specifications and COA?
Yes — full grade specification sheets and batch-by-batch COA (exact Al2O3, Fe, basicity and impurity values) come with every shipment and are available before ordering. Contact us with your required spec and we will confirm match and send the datasheet the same day.
Is PAFC safe for drinking water applications?
PAFC is primarily used in industrial and non-potable applications. For drinking water treatment we recommend our GB 15892-2020 drinking water grade PAC powder, which carries strict heavy-metal limits — we supply both and will guide you to the compliant product for each use.
