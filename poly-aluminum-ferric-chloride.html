Product Overview

Two Coagulants in One Product

PAFC is a composite aluminum-iron coagulant used in water and wastewater treatment. The balance of aluminum and iron components varies by product form and grade. Jar testing is recommended to compare PAFC with PAC or other coagulants under the actual operating conditions.

PAFC may be evaluated for water with changing turbidity or color, but performance depends on the raw-water matrix and selected grade. Taixinchang coordinates sourcing and specification matching; product form, packaging and supporting documents are confirmed for each inquiry.

Applications

Where PAFC Works Best

Surface Water Treatment

High-turbidity river and reservoir water — one product for both monsoon and dry season.

Textile & Dyeing

Strong decolorization where aluminum-only coagulants leave residual color.

Municipal Wastewater

Solids and phosphorus removal with dense, fast-settling flocs.

Industrial Process Water

Clarification where heavy metals need co-precipitation alongside solids removal.
