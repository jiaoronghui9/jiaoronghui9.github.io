Product Overview

Nonionic PAM for Special Applications

Nonionic polyacrylamide (NPAM) carries no ionic charge in solution, making it effective in acidic or variable-pH systems where charged polymers lose efficiency. Typical uses include textile warp sizing, paper mill retention and drainage aids, and flocculation of acidic wastewater or mineral slurries with high salinity.

We supply four molecular weight grades (6 to 12 million) with hydrolysis degree below 5%, produced by long-term partner factories in Gongyi and shipped worldwide with COA per batch. Free samples available for lab evaluation before bulk ordering.

FAQ

Nonionic PAM — Common Questions

When should I choose nonionic instead of anionic PAM?
Choose nonionic when your process water is strongly acidic (pH below 4) or has high salinity that suppresses ionic polymer performance, or for non-treatment uses like textile sizing and paper retention. If you are unsure, send us your water data — our technical team will compare grades for you.
Do you support trial orders?
Yes. MOQ is only 1 ton for first orders, and free samples (500g-1kg) are available before that — you cover only the courier fee. Many customers start with samples, run jar tests, then order a trial ton before scaling to full containers.
How is nonionic PAM packed for export?
Standard packing is 25kg kraft paper bags with PE inner liner (20 tons per 20' container) or 1000kg jumbo bags. Custom-printed or neutral packaging is available — neutral bags are popular with trading companies.
