Product Overview

PAC Liquid — Product Information

Liquid PAC is considered where the treatment process uses direct liquid dosing. Its Al₂O₃ content, density, basicity, insoluble matter and pH must be assessed as liquid-product parameters and must not be copied from solid PAC specifications.

Indicative Parameters

ParameterIndicative information
Al₂O₃ ContentApprox. ≥10%
BasicityApprox. 40–90%
Density (20°C)Approx. ≥1.12 g/cm³ in the referenced supplier specification
Water-insoluble matterApprox. ≤0.2% in the referenced supplier specification
pH (10 g/L solution)Approx. 3.5–5.0

The above are indicative values from the supplier's general liquid PAC table. Actual concentration, density, packaging, shelf life and batch limits must be confirmed for the selected supplier and order.

FAQ

PAC Liquid — Common Questions

Is liquid PAC the same as PAC powder?
No. Liquid PAC has a separate concentration, density, storage and packaging specification. Solid PAC values must not be applied to liquid PAC.
What concentration can you supply?
Supplier literature identifies liquid PAC from approximately 10% Al₂O₃. The current grade and concentration are confirmed for each inquiry.
What details are needed for a quotation?
Please share daily or monthly consumption, dosing arrangement, storage conditions, destination and target Al₂O₃ content.
